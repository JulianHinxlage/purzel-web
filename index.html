Where are my photos stored?

Only on your iPhone. Purzel has no account and no cloud, so your photos, journey and companion never leave your device. Deleting the app also deletes them, so keep the originals in your photo library.

Why does Purzel ask for my location?

To find out where a photo was taken: the city, the country and nearby landmarks. Location is only used while you take a photo, never in the background. You can also say no and enter the place yourself.

What's included for free, and what does Plus add?

The free plan includes 20 photos with all features. Purzel Plus makes photos unlimited, as a yearly or monthly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase.

How do I restore a purchase on a new phone?

Open Settings → Plan → Upgrade to Plus in Purzel and tap Restore purchases at the bottom. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you bought Plus with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. In Purzel, tap Settings → Manage subscription, or open the iPhone Settings app → your name → Subscriptions. Plus stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I turn off notifications?

In Purzel, go to Settings → Notifications. You can switch off trip recaps and memories separately, or all notifications at once.

How do I delete my data?

Use Settings → Reset all data in Purzel, or delete the app. To have anonymous usage statistics deleted as well, email us and we'll take care of it. See the privacy policy for details.
